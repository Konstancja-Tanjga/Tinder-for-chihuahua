Double opt-in, rewritten for these two sides

Step 06 of the first journey and step 04 of this one are two halves of the same mechanism. The dog’s swipe is the first consent and the human’s approval is the second — and only together do they open the way to a meeting. The pattern comes straight from Tinder and Bumble; only the parties have changed.
