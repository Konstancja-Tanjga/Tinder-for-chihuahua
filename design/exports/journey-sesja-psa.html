What the empty cells are telling you

Four steps happen in the room, but the screen only actually falls silent in two of them — 01 and 08, where the “Screen” row has a dash. The first is setting the scene. The second is the reward — which means the single most important step in the entire loop is one where the app’s job is to sit there and do nothing. All it did was announce the treat back in step 07.
